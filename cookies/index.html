Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

Nope. This website does not use cookies.

No analytics cookies. No advertising cookies. No tracking pixels. No social widgets following you around the web. Why would we stalk you?

That is also why there is no cookie popup. There is nothing useful for you to accept, reject, manage, dismiss, or swear at.

Local browser storage

The site may store your language preference in your browser using local storage. That is not a cookie, and it is not sent to us. It only helps the site remember which language you picked.

You can clear it at any time from your browser's site data settings.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or similar tracking technology, we will update this page first and ask properly where consent is required.

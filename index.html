Tone Architecture

TayPE isn't a flat digital mixer. Every channel runs through a modelled preamp stage — real hardware character captured by Neural Amp Modeler and applied at the source. On the mixbus, a modelled summing engine adds console-style saturation, cohesion, and drive.

No plugin instances. No sandboxed processes. The console emulation runs directly in the audio engine — shared weight tensors, per-channel state, zero added latency. Same philosophy as hardware-accelerated DSP, built into the mixer, not bolted on.

Craft first. Tone at the core.

Modelled Preamps

Per-channel hardware character from captured NAM profiles — Neve, API, SSL, and more via the TONE3000 community library. Shape tone at the source and commit with intention. Three modes: clean gain, built-in saturator, or full neural amp model.

Modelled Console Summing

A NAM-powered summing engine on the master bus. Load a captured console profile and every signal hits the bus with real nonlinear character — saturation, harmonic colour, and subtle compression that responds to dynamics. What you hear is what sums.

True Summing

Coming soon: per-channel NAM processing before the sum — every track through its own console channel, just like pushing 24 signals through a real analog desk. Shared weights, batched matrix inference on Apple Silicon. Full console emulation at a fraction of the CPU cost.

Make the Record

TayPE exists to help you finish real takes, not drown in endless options. A multitrack DAW with the soul of a tape machine. Fewer modes, fewer knobs, more truth.

Built from the ground up with accessibility at its core. WCAG 2.2 AA compliant. High-contrast mode. Keyboard navigable. Because musicians are not all sighted, all hearing, or all able-bodied.

Accessible by Design

WCAG 2.2 AA. High-contrast theme. Full keyboard navigation. Screen-reader compatible labels. Designed for every musician, not just the ones who can see 12px text on a dark background.

MCP Integration

Optional machine assistance for batch edits and session management. Automate the admin, not the art. TayPE remains deterministic and transactional at its core — MCP enhances workflow without replacing craft.

Focus Over Clutter

Focus mode shows only what matters at the playhead. Archive mode silences dead tracks completely - no CPU, no noise. Commit over endless looping.

Native Console Channel Strip

Neve-style 3-band EQ, HP/LP filters, compressor with log release, and 4 VST3 insert slots per track. Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Multitrack Truth

Record, arrange, and mix with a clean signal path. Real-time safe audio engine. No hidden latency, no surprise. What you hear is what you get.

SSL 360° Integration

Full SSL 360° support with bidirectional fader and pan control via UF8/UC1. Track names, colours, and layout push to your surface automatically. No mapping, no config — just plug in and mix.

Mix FX — Softube Tape

Dedicated tape saturation slot on the mix bus, powered by Softube Multitrack Tape. One knob away from analogue warmth on your stereo bus. No insert chain fiddling — just enable and print.

Stems, Not Folders

Stems are real summing buses. One layer deep. No nested folder hell. Your console layout stays stable and predictable.

From practice to place

Technology should disappear, so the music can take centre stage.

Recording software has become more powerful, but often at the expense of clarity. More menus. More modes. More decisions before the first note is even recorded.

We build in the opposite direction: fewer distractions, stronger defaults, and tools that quietly support the creative process.

TayPE carries forward the parts of analogue recording that genuinely helped people make better records: immediacy, focus, confidence, and musical momentum.

Meet the founder

I'm Tom Taylor, founder of Ruminant Audio Works. I've spent my life performing, recording, engineering, and obsessing over the details that make records connect.

TayPE is the distillation of that journey: a recording environment built around commitment, flow, and the feeling that the machine is helping rather than asking to be managed.

If you've found your way here, thank you for giving TayPE a look. I hope it helps you make something you're proud of.

Tom Taylor

Make the Record

Does this help someone make a better record?

If the answer is yes, we're interested. If it adds complexity for its own sake, we're probably not. That's the philosophy behind TayPE, and it will continue to guide everything we build.

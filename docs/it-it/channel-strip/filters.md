# Filtri (HP/LP)

High-pass and low-pass filters for cleaning up the frequency extremes. These avere their own bypass toggle, separate da the EQ.

| Filtro | Default | Range |
|--------|---------|-------|
| High-pass | 30 Hz | 20 - 1,000 Hz |
| Low-pass | 18,000 Hz | 2,000 - 20,000 Hz |

The high-pass is your rumble killer. The low-pass tames harshness up top.
Both are 12 dB/oct second-order curves con a small fixed resonant lift at
cutoff, so they feel a bit more alive than dead-flat Butterworth filters.

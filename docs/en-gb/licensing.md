# Licensing

TayPE uses a trustware licence model. Try TayPE properly. If it becomes part of how you make music, buy a perpetual licence. That keeps TayPE breaking new ground, and bugs squashed.

## Licence tiers

- **Personal — £75:** non-commercial use, including non-monetised publishing.
- **Commercial — £150:** commercial publishing, monetised releases (including your own music), paid client work, work-for-hire, paid production services, and use on behalf of studios, businesses, labels and organisations.

Both tiers include the full application with identical functionality and updates for the current major version (Era) and the next. The difference is the permitted use. If your use becomes commercial, obtain a Commercial licence before continuing that use. Both tiers also include unrestricted use of TayPE's stock plug-ins in other DAWs.

To upgrade from Personal to Commercial, email [concessions@ruminantaudioworks.com](mailto:concessions@ruminantaudioworks.com).

Use TayPE alongside your existing DAW, or make the whole record here. See the [licence agreement](https://taype.ruminantaudioworks.com/terms/) for the full terms.

## Concessions

If you really want to use TayPE but can’t afford our prices, drop me a line at [concessions@ruminantaudioworks.com](mailto:concessions@ruminantaudioworks.com). We may be able to sort something out.

## Trustware Demo

TayPE is fully functional from its first launch. The licensing window shows how many days are left in the 90-day evaluation and can be closed immediately during that period. After 90 days, the reminder shown when you launch TayPE or open another reel has a ten-second dismissal countdown. Audio export remains available.

Stock plug-ins in other DAWs share TayPE's 90-day evaluation. After it ends, their editors display a dismissible **Buy TayPE** banner when opened. The controls remain usable.

## Agreement

A licence supports continued development, maintenance, signing, distribution, and support. Keep your licence email somewhere safe; TayPE can recover existing licences through the support path when needed.
